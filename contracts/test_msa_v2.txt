MASTER SERVICE AGREEMENT

This Master Service Agreement ("Agreement") is made as of October 1, 2026 (the "Effective Date") between Software Solutions Inc. ("Provider") and Enterprise Corp. ("Client").

1. TERM
The initial term shall be three (3) years from the Effective Date. The Agreement shall automatically renew for successive one-year periods unless either party provides written notice of non-renewal no later than ninety (90) days before the end of the current term.

2. SERVICES
Provider shall provide cloud infrastructure services including hosting, maintenance, and 24/7 support.

3. FEES AND PAYMENT
3.1 Service Fee: Client shall pay Provider a monthly fee of $25,000 USD.
3.2 Payment Terms: Net 45 days from invoice date.
3.3 Late Payment: Late payments accrue interest at 2% per month, not to exceed the maximum rate allowed by law.

4. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY
All Provider technology and pre-existing IP remains Provider's property. Custom developments shall be jointly owned.

5. WARRANTY AND LIMITATION OF LIABILITY
5.1 Warranty Disclaimer: Services provided AS-IS, without warranties of any kind.
5.2 Limitation: Neither party's liability shall exceed the fees paid in the preceding 12 months.
5.3 Indemnification: Each party shall indemnify the other against third-party claims arising from its breach or negligence.

6. CONFIDENTIALITY
Confidential information disclosed under this Agreement shall remain confidential for five (5) years following termination.

7. TERMINATION
Either party may terminate for material breach if not cured within thirty (30) days of written notice. Either party may terminate for convenience with sixty (60) days' notice.

8. GOVERNING LAW
This Agreement is governed by Delaware law. Disputes shall be resolved by arbitration in Wilmington, Delaware.

9. ASSIGNMENT
Neither party may assign without written consent, except Provider may assign to a successor in a merger or acquisition.

10. SERVICE LEVELS
Provider shall maintain 99.9% uptime. If availability falls below 99.9% in any month, Client receives a 5% service credit for that month.
