# Non-Disclosure Agreement

## 1. Parties

This Non-Disclosure Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of September 7, 2026 ("Effective Date") by and between TechVenture Inc., a California corporation ("Disclosing Party"), and SecureData LLC, a Texas limited liability company ("Receiving Party").

## 2. Confidential Information

Confidential Information means any non-public information disclosed by the Disclosing Party, including but not limited to: technical data, business plans, financial information, customer lists, source code, and trade secrets.

## 3. Obligations

The Receiving Party agrees to:
(a) Keep Confidential Information strictly confidential
(b) Use Confidential Information only for the purpose of evaluating a potential business relationship
(c) Protect Confidential Information with reasonable care
(d) Not disclose to third parties without prior written consent

## 4. Term and Survival

This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and continue for three (3) years. Confidentiality obligations shall survive termination indefinitely for trade secrets.

## 5. Return of Information

Upon termination or upon request, Receiving Party shall promptly return or destroy all Confidential Information.

## 6. No License

Nothing in this Agreement grants any license or rights to Confidential Information except as expressly stated.

## 7. Governing Law

This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of California.

## 8. Entire Agreement

This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties with respect to the subject matter.
