# Statement of Work

## 1. Overview

This Statement of Work ("SOW") is entered into as of September 7, 2026 by and between Acme Consulting LLC ("Vendor") and Design Studio Inc. ("Client"), pursuant to the Master Service Agreement dated January 15, 2026.

## 2. Scope of Services

Vendor shall provide the following services:
- UI/UX design for mobile application redesign (40 hours)
- Creation of high-fidelity mockups and prototypes (30 hours)
- User research and testing (20 hours)
- Design system documentation (10 hours)

Total estimated effort: 100 hours

## 3. Deliverables

1. Research findings report (by October 15, 2026)
2. Low-fidelity wireframes (by October 25, 2026)
3. High-fidelity mockups (by November 15, 2026)
4. Interactive prototype (by November 30, 2026)
5. Design system guide and assets (by December 15, 2026)

## 4. Project Timeline

Project start: September 15, 2026
Project end: December 15, 2026
Estimated duration: 13 weeks

## 5. Compensation

Total project fee: $15,000 USD

Payment schedule:
- 50% ($7,500) upon execution of SOW
- 50% ($7,500) upon final deliverable acceptance

Payment terms: Net 30 from invoice date

## 6. Approval Process

Client shall review each deliverable within 5 business days. Feedback shall be provided in writing. Vendor shall implement requested revisions within 3 business days.

## 7. Change Orders

Any scope changes must be documented in a written change order signed by both parties. Additional work beyond the scope listed above will be billed at $150/hour.

## 8. Acceptance Criteria

Deliverables are considered accepted when Client provides written sign-off or does not provide feedback within 5 business days of delivery.

## 9. Confidentiality

All deliverables and work products are the property of Client. Vendor shall maintain confidentiality of all Client information for 3 years following project completion.

## 10. Termination

Client may terminate this SOW with 5 business days written notice. If terminated, Vendor shall invoice Client for all work completed through the termination date on a time-and-materials basis.

## 11. Limitation of Liability

Neither party's liability shall exceed the total fees paid under this SOW ($15,000).

## 12. Governing Law

This SOW shall be governed by the laws of California.
